Panel 06What these numbers rest on

Unverified inputs

The geometry behind every position and residual here comes from a cache built outside O2, and its alignment-delta convention has not been checked against an O2 reference. Two constants in the impact-parameter cut — Angle2Alpha and B2C — were reconstructed rather than copied from O2 source, and they decide which tracks enter the cost at all.

The seed weights are from step 900, dated 2022, while the data is run 539884 from 2023. Some of what appears here as misalignment may simply be that gap.

Panel 05 shows a d0(z) distribution that is bimodal — a core of a few hundred µm carrying about half the tracks, and a nearly flat pedestal out to ±6 cm carrying the rest — while d0(rφ) from the same tracks is a clean single peak with an RMS that falls as 1/pT. A transverse fit that behaves and a longitudinal one that does not points at the z step rather than at the geometry: z_dca is evaluated by extrapolating the z–β line to the β of the closest-approach point, so a β branch taken the wrong way around the circle moves z by an arbitrary amount without touching rφ. That is a reading of the shape, not a diagnosis — it has not been traced in the code.

Treat this page as a way to see structure, not as a validated measurement.
